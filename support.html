PCSP (working name) is an iPhone app for practicing the practical driving test at the Samezu test center in Tokyo, the test taken when converting a foreign driver’s license (外免切替). You drive the test courses in 3D and see where you would lose points. It is an independent practice tool, not affiliated with the police or any test center, and the real test may differ.

Contact us

Email menkyohub@gmail.com. If you are reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Restore purchases

If a purchase is not showing, open the app and go to Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose the subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple: Request a refund from Apple.
